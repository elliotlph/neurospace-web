Non-monogamy is a skill set, not a personality test

Polyamory and ethical non-monogamy can be deeply good for people. They can also surface every insecurity, communication gap and unspoken expectation at once. None of that means you're doing it wrong. It means you're doing something most of us never had a model for.

  • You're thinking about opening your relationship and don't know where to start
  • One of you is poly and one is monogamous, and you're trying to make it work
  • Jealousy, comparison or insecurity keeps hijacking the good stuff
  • Your agreements were made in a hurry and don't fit anymore
  • Your polycule needs a neutral space to sort out conflict, time and boundaries
What we'd work on

What working with me looks like

Opening up

Getting clear on why you want this, what each of you actually needs, and how to start without blowing up what you already have.

Agreements and boundaries

Building agreements that are honest, flexible and revisitable, and figuring out the difference between a boundary and a rule.

Jealousy and insecurity

Looking at what jealousy is pointing to instead of treating it as a stop sign or a personal failure.

Mono-poly relationships

Respecting both relationship orientations without one person quietly carrying all the compromise.

Polycule coaching

Group sessions for multi-partner relationships working through communication breakdowns, conflicting needs and logistics.

Neurodivergence and ENM

Calendars, capacity, rejection sensitivity and how your brain shapes the way you love, without shame.

Clients in the U.S.: I work with U.S.-based clients as a coach only. I no longer practise as a licensed therapist, and our work isn't therapy or counselling under U.S. state licensing.

Questions

Common questions

Do I have to already be polyamorous to work with you?

No. Plenty of people come to me while they're still curious, and some decide non-monogamy isn't for them. Every relationship style gets the same respect here.

Can my partners join sessions?

Yes. I work with individuals, couples and polycules, so sessions can be solo, with one partner, or with several people at once.

Will you try to talk us into or out of non-monogamy?

No. My job is to help you get clear on what you want and build the skills to do it well, whatever structure that ends up being.
