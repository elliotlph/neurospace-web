Approach

My Coaching Approach

I see myself as someone who stands alongside people through the real complexity of life— not above it, not outside it.

My approach is evidence-based, intuitive, and creative. I think of coaching as a kind of alchemy: multiple things have to come together to create genuine change. For me, those are authenticity, agency, and autonomy. Whatever you're bringing to this work, those three things are the foundation.

Let's talk and see where it goes.

The foundation

So what’s this all about?

Authenticity

Okay, I know this word can sound corny or over-done but I really do believe that when we can access our most genuine self, we are able to experience life in a much richer color. And when I say authentic, I don’t mean not caring about what other people think, I just mean being as true to yourself as possible in any given moment.

Agency

I use this word a lot because it’s at the core of what I believe causes suffering: a lack or stripping away of agency. When we don’t feel like active and consenting participants in our lives, we experience immense emotional and mental distress. By my logic, reclaiming agency is key to growth.

Autonomy

Similar but different than agency, it’s imperative in my opinion, that we foster access to our own autonomy. Not in the sense of individualism, but rather the experience of being a differentiated but interdependent self that coexists with others. When we feel autonomous, we actually have more capacity for connection and a felt sense of safety.
