Sound familiar?

Love across languages, families and ideas of home

Multicultural relationships bring a lot of richness, and a lot to negotiate: different expectations about family, money, roles and conflict, often in a language that isn't one partner's first. Add a move to a new country and things get heavier. I'm in a cross-cultural relationship and relocated to Berlin myself, so I know this from the inside.

  • You keep clashing over family, holidays, money or roles and can't tell what's cultural and what's personal
  • One of you moved for the other, and the balance of the relationship has shifted
  • Something important keeps getting lost in translation
  • You moved to Germany and the bureaucracy, loneliness and in-between identity are wearing you down
  • You're deciding where to build your life long-term
What we'd work on

What working with me looks like

Cultural expectations

Naming the assumptions each of you grew up with so you can decide together what you want to keep.

Family and in-laws

Navigating family across borders, time zones and very different ideas of closeness and obligation.

Communication across languages

Building ways to talk about hard things when you don't share a first language.

Relocation and imbalance

Working through what changes when one partner leaves their country, career or community for the relationship.

Belonging as an immigrant

Finding your footing, your people and your identity in a new place, whether you moved last month or years ago.

Big decisions

Where to live, whether to stay, and how to plan a future that works for both of you.

How it works

Online, in English, at your pace

  • Sessions are online by video, so you can join from Berlin, anywhere in Germany or Europe, the UK, or North America.
  • Sessions are in English, Monday–Thursday, 2pm–9pm CET.
  • Fees follow a sliding scale (€100–€160 per session, adjusted to your local currency).
  • We start with a free consultation to see whether working together feels right.

Coaching and counselling aren't psychotherapy, and I don't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you need clinical care, I'll tell you directly and help you think about where to find it.

Clients in the U.S.: I work with U.S.-based clients as a coach only. I no longer practise as a licensed therapist, and our work isn't therapy or counselling under U.S. state licensing.

Questions

Common questions

Do you work only with couples living in Berlin?

No. Sessions are online, so I work with couples and individuals across Germany, Europe, the UK and North America.

We speak different first languages. Is English OK?

Sessions are in English. Many of my clients speak English as a second or third language, and we'll go at whatever pace works.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Individual coaching and counselling for immigrants and people adjusting to a new country is a big part of what I do.
