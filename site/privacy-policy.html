Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Neurospace Coaching collects only the personal data needed to run this website, answer your questions and provide coaching and counselling services. We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.

This policy explains what we collect when you visit neurospacecoaching.com, contact us or become a client, why we collect it, who helps us process it, and the rights you have. It applies to visitors and clients in the EU, the UK, the United States and anywhere else we work.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Neurospace Coaching. "You" means anyone who visits the site or uses our services.

Sensitive information

Coaching and counselling conversations often involve sensitive topics, such as your health, neurodivergence, sexual orientation, gender identity or relationship structure. Under GDPR these are "special categories" of data. We process them only with your explicit consent, given in your client agreement, and only to provide the services you asked for. You can withdraw that consent at any time.

Please don't send sensitive details in your first email. A short note about what you're looking for is enough; we can talk about the rest in your free consultation.

4. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, retargeting or social media tracking. Fonts are hosted on our own server, so your browser doesn't contact third-party font services when you visit.

7. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can email elliot@neurospacecoaching.com to exercise any of these rights. We answer within one month and don't charge for it. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you're in the EU or UK

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you and get a copy
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your data, unless we must keep it by law
  • Restrict how we use your data
  • Port your data to another provider in a common format
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting anything done before
  • Complain to a data protection authority

Our lead authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (datenschutz-berlin.de). UK residents can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). You can also complain to the authority where you live or work.

If you're in the United States

We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we never have. Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what we collect, to access or delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of sale or targeted advertising. We extend these rights to all U.S. visitors and clients, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

8. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Client records are kept in password-protected, access-restricted systems that only Elliot can open, and video sessions run on an encrypted platform. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our services, providers or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change significantly affects how we use client data, we will tell current clients directly.
