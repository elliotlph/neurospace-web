Sound familiar?

Your relationship doesn't have to fit anyone else's script

Queer relationships come with their own strengths and their own pressures: family who don't get it, gender and identity shifting over time, chosen family, and few models for what a good relationship can look like. I bring both professional and personal experience to this work, so you can spend sessions on your life, not educating your coach.

  • You keep having the same fight and can't find what's underneath it
  • One of you is exploring gender or transitioning, and you're figuring out what it means for you both
  • Coming out, family tension or homophobia and transphobia are spilling into your relationship
  • You want to strengthen your relationship before it's in crisis
  • You're questioning your identity and want space to explore it
What we'd work on

What working with me looks like

Communication and conflict

Mapping the conflict cycle you're stuck in and building practical ways to hear each other.

Gender and transition in relationships

Support for couples navigating a partner's gender exploration or transition, with room for both of your experiences.

Identity and coming out

Exploring who you are, coming out (or not) on your own timeline, and handling the reactions.

Family and chosen family

Setting boundaries with family of origin and building the community you actually want.

Intimacy and connection

Reconnecting when life, stress or old stuff has created distance between you.

Relationship structure

Monogamous, open, polyamorous or something else: non-monogamy coaching is part of what I do too.

How it works

Online, in English, at your pace

  • Sessions are online by video, so you can join from Berlin, anywhere in Germany or Europe, the UK, or North America.
  • Sessions are in English, Monday–Thursday, 2pm–9pm CET.
  • Fees follow a sliding scale (€100–€160 per session, adjusted to your local currency).
  • We start with a free consultation to see whether working together feels right.

Coaching and counselling aren't psychotherapy, and I don't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you need clinical care, I'll tell you directly and help you think about where to find it.

Clients in the U.S.: I work with U.S.-based clients as a coach only. I no longer practise as a licensed therapist, and our work isn't therapy or counselling under U.S. state licensing.

Questions

Common questions

Do you only work with queer couples?

My practice centers LGBTQ+ people, and I also work with individuals and with straight couples where one partner is queer, trans or questioning.

Is this couples therapy?

It's coaching and couples counselling, which means we focus on where you want to go and how to get there rather than diagnosing or treating anything. If you need couples therapy, I'll say so.

Can we do sessions from different locations?

Yes. Sessions are online, so you can join together or from separate places.
