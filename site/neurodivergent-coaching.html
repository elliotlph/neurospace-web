You're not lazy, broken or too much

Whether you've known you're neurodivergent for years, only recently realised it, or are still exploring the question, coaching here starts from how you actually function, not how you've been told you should. I don't diagnose, but I can help you understand your patterns and build a life that fits them.

  • Executive functioning keeps getting in the way of things you genuinely want to do
  • You're burnt out from masking at work, with family, or in your relationship
  • You were identified as an adult and are rethinking a lot of your story
  • Your relationship keeps snagging on communication, chores, sensory needs or time
  • Productivity advice makes you feel worse, not better
What we'd work on

What working with me looks like

Executive functioning

Practical, brain-friendly systems for the dishes, the inbox, the appointments and the projects you keep meaning to start.

Burnout and recovery

Figuring out what's draining you, what you can stop carrying, and how to protect your energy going forward.

Masking and unmasking

Exploring who you are under the masking and deciding, on your terms, how you want to show up.

Neurodivergent relationships

Coaching for couples where one or both partners are neurodivergent: communication styles, sensory needs, emotional load and conflict.

Work and self-advocacy

Navigating being neurodivergent at work, asking for what you need, or finding work that doesn't flatten you.

Identity

Making sense of a late identification and how it intersects with being queer, trans, an immigrant, or all of the above.

How it works

Online, in English, at your pace

  • Sessions are online by video, so you can join from Berlin, anywhere in Germany or Europe, the UK, or North America.
  • Sessions are in English, Monday–Thursday, 2pm–9pm CET.
  • Fees follow a sliding scale (€100–€160 per session, adjusted to your local currency).
  • We start with a free consultation to see whether working together feels right.

Coaching and counselling aren't psychotherapy, and I don't diagnose or treat mental health conditions. If you need clinical care, I'll tell you directly and help you think about where to find it.

Clients in the U.S.: I work with U.S.-based clients as a coach only. I no longer practise as a licensed therapist, and our work isn't therapy or counselling under U.S. state licensing.

Common questions

Do I need a diagnosis to work with you?

No. Self-identified, formally diagnosed, or just wondering, you're welcome here. Coaching doesn't require or provide a diagnosis.

Can we work on this as a couple?

Yes. I work with couples where one or both partners are neurodivergent, and with polycules too.
