Sliding Scale

Fee Structure & Cost

Session cost operates based on a sliding scale structure determined by client-reported income.

Bracket A

Constrained

Enough to cover essentials, utilities, and basic bills. Savings are minimal or non-existent. Prioritizing needs over wants.

Single income household
€100 – €125
Two income household
€115 – €130
Bracket B

Comfortable

Income covers living expenses, bills, and leaves a buffer for savings or discretionary spending. Low to moderate budgeting stress.

Single income household
€125 – €135
Two income household
€130 – €145
Bracket C

Secure

Income exceeds living expenses. Savings and investments are a possible, allowing for lifestyle flexibility and low financial stress.

Single income household
€140 – €155
Two income household
€150 – €160

The best approach is to use these ranges as a starting point rather than rigid requirements. Consider your personal situation and talk with me directly if you have questions or concerns.

**I work with clients across the US, UK, and EU. Fees are adjusted accordingly to your local currency.
